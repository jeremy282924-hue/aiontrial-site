Privacy policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026.

AI on Trial Publisher is an internal tool used only by the AI on Trial team to upload our own short videos to our own YouTube channel and to read our own channel's basic information.

What it accesses

  • YouTube upload permission for our own channel (to publish our videos).
  • Read-only access to our own channel (to confirm uploads).

What it does not do

  • It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.
  • It does not collect, sell or share personal data. Access tokens are stored encrypted on our own computer and are never shared.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact: see the @aiontrial.lab profile.
